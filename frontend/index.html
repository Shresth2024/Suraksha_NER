WHY SURAKSHA NER?

A real-world problem needs more than another alert.

The North Eastern Region faces recurring hazards such as intense rainfall, floods and landslides. Hilly and remote areas can experience rapidly changing local conditions, difficult terrain, road disruption and communication challenges. Existing disaster-management systems already provide important warnings; the challenge is making risk information easier to understand at the person + location + moment level.

01

Risk changes locally

Risk can vary sharply between nearby areas. SURAKSHA NER is designed around location-specific risk rather than one generic message for everyone.

02

Warnings need context

Users should understand what hazard is developing, why risk is rising and what practical action they can take.

03

People move

A person travelling between states should not remain tied to an old saved location. The product is designed for continuously updated location-aware alerts.

04

Response must be actionable

Alongside an alert, people need quick access to emergency contacts, safety guidance and navigation toward an appropriate destination.
