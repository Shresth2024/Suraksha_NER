EMERGENCY INFORMATION

Safety guide

Simple actions for common hazards.

⛰️

Landslide

  • Move away from steep slopes and unstable ground.
  • Avoid crossing debris, mudflow or blocked roads.
  • Follow local authority evacuation instructions.
  • Keep emergency communication available.
🌊

Flood

  • Move to safer higher ground when advised.
  • Do not walk or drive through fast-moving floodwater.
  • Stay away from damaged electrical infrastructure.
  • Follow verified local warnings.
🌧️

Very heavy rainfall

  • Limit unnecessary travel during severe conditions.
  • Monitor official weather and disaster warnings.
  • Keep power banks, water and essentials ready.
  • Check on vulnerable family members when safe.
IMPORTANT

Official warnings come first

SURAKSHA NER is a prototype. During a real emergency, follow authorized government and emergency services.
